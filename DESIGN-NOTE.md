# Design Note — Firefly Jar

## Why I chose my added mechanic

**Sleepy fireflies — the game demonstrates itself.** Untouched for ~9 seconds,
one firefly at a time gets sleepy (the charsheet's sleepy face), dims, and
drifts down into the jar; left alone, the night completes the round by itself.
Any touch wakes everyone, and tapping a dozing firefly catches it with a
surprised "oh!".

Why this one: it's a wordless tutorial — a 4-year-old who doesn't know what to
do watches a firefly do it. And it fits the brief's scale: one behavior on
existing objects, no new inputs, no new rules, and it deepens the bedtime calm
instead of fighting it.

## What I'd build next

1. **Color personalities** — yellow steady drifters, pink dreamy loopers, teal
   playful zig-zaggers (all inside the calm caps). *Learning goal:* observation
   and prediction — watch first, notice patterns, anticipate where a friend goes.
2. **Color-matched jars** — two or three jars, each glowing softly in the color
   it wishes for; any firefly in any jar counts, a match earns an extra sparkle.
   *Learning goal:* sorting and classification, with zero fail state.

## What I deliberately cut

- A "shy firefly" coaxing mechanic — needed hint systems a pre-reader would
  never discover; over-scoped.
- A 3D art pipeline — the brief says flat; spec fidelity beat spectacle.
- Any score, numbers, menus, or text — progress stays physical: lights in a jar.
